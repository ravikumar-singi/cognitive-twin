How Cognitive Twin works

  1. A model of you. Every answer updates how sure the tutors are that you know each skill.
  2. Three tutors, one goal. The planner picks the skill, the question-picker chooses difficulty and style, and the pacer suggests breaks. They learned to teach by practising on thousands of simulated students.
  3. A safety guardian. Rules the tutors can't break: no locked skills, no stretch questions for beginners, and regular breaks (more often for younger learners: at least every 12 questions under 13, 18 for teens, 25 for adults).
  4. Credentials you can prove. When you master a skill, validators check your answers and sign a credential anyone can verify.
  5. Your data, your choice. Your school decides who can join and confirms a parent's or guardian's agreement for under-18s. You can download your data at any time; adults can delete it themselves, and teachers or parents can delete a child's. We keep only a first name for young children, never show adverts, and the focus headband is not used.
  6. Several subjects. Middle-school maths, high-school algebra, Python basics, general science and machine-learning foundations, each with its own trained tutors. Teachers should review the questions before using them with a class.
