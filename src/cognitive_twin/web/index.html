How Cognitive Twin works

    
    
      
  1. A model of you. Every answer updates how sure the tutors are that you know each skill.
    2. 
      
  2. Three tutors, one goal. The planner picks the skill, the question-picker chooses difficulty and style, and the pacer calls breaks. They learned how to teach by practising on thousands of simulated students.
    3. 
      
  3. A safety guardian. Simple rules the tutors can't break: no locked skills, no stretch questions for beginners, and a break at least every 25 questions.
    4. 
      
  4. Credentials you can prove. When you master a skill, independent validators check your answers and sign a credential anyone can verify.
    5. 
      
  5. Your privacy. The headband is optional and simulated here. Brain signals stay on the device, they never affect your credentials, and the headband is always off for under-16s and in the EU.
